What sells and what earns - category, product, channel and discount.

Actionable insights

- Software is the profit engine: 13.25 M revenue at 77.7% margin, nearly half of revenue and the bulk of profit.

- Furniture is the drag - 5.08 M at 54.0% margin across 33 orders. Most orders, least return. Candidate to reprice or deprioritise.

- Analytics Suite is the standout product: 6.03 M at 80.1% margin, effectively matching Laptop Pro's volume (6.21 M) at far better economics.

- Channels are close to even (Direct 10.34 M, Partner 8.77 M, Online 8.62 M) with margins within two points, so channel is not where the leverage is.

- Discount signal is counter-intuitive: 1-5% discounts show the highest margin (70.6%) and the most revenue (10.17 M), better than no discount at all (66.9%). A small discount closes better deals, not worse ones.

Actions: cap discounts at 5%, push Analytics Suite attach, and review the Furniture line.
