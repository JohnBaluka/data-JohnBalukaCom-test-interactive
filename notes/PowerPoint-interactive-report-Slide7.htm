How the year is tracking - monthly trend and movers.

Actionable insights

- The year is volatile, not trending: peaks in April (5.68 M) and June (4.74 M) against troughs in May (2.09 M), July (2.51 M) and August (2.11 M).

- The last three months are the weakest stretch of the year - roughly 2.2 M a month against a 3.5 M average. Treat this as a pipeline warning, not seasonality.

- Margin is also eroding: 71.6% in April down to 65.0% in August. Volume and quality are falling together, which points to mix rather than pricing.

- April alone is 20% of the year's revenue. Understand what made April work and whether it was repeatable or one large deal.

Ask of the room: is the H2 softness a pipeline gap or a coverage gap? The answer changes the fix.
